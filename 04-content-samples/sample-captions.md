# 04 · Sample Content — English Captions（英文文案样例）

> 这三条是**可直接发布**的英文文案样例（配图怎么做见 `visual-blueprints.md`）。
> 写文案的套路：**钩子开头 + 情绪价值 + 互动引导 + 分层标签**。每条都经过我本人撰写与审改。

## Post 1 — Unboxing（开箱 · 图文 / 视频封面）

```
you know it's a good day when the package is THIS soft 🧸
unboxing my new squishy best friend — say hi to Mochi!
📦 What's the last thing you bought just because it was too cute? 👇

#plushie #unboxing #cute #tiktokmademebuyit #emotionalsupportplush #plushieparadise
```

## Post 2 — Cozy Moment（治愈 · 视频）

```
POV: your plushie checks in on you after a long day 🥺✨
some things just make everything softer.
save this for your next rainy day 🌧️

#cozyvibes #plushcorner #stuffedanimal #selfcare #plushieparadise
```

## Post 3 — Meme / Mini-skit（玩梗 · 视频）

```
my plushie judging my 3am snack choices like 👀🍪
rate my plushie's reaction from 1 to 10 (he takes this very seriously)

#plushiememes #funnyvideos #pov #plushieparadise #cute
```

## 发布小贴士

- 首条评论自己补一句引导（如 *"Which one should I unbox next? 🧸"*），并置顶；
- 发布后 30 分钟内回复所有评论——前 1 小时互动率决定推流；
- 同一内容二发 Instagram 时，把标签换成 IG 常用词（#aesthetic #roomdecor 等）。
